My Politics Program

Six practical initiatives to make Politics more engaging, interactive, and student-driven.

1

THE DEBATE CLUB

Organize debates where students can share opinions, defend their ideas, and discuss interesting topics.

2

MINI PARLIAMENT

Create a school parliament where students discuss school issues, suggest solutions, and vote on ideas.

3

YOUR VOICE MATTERS

Introduce an anonymous suggestion box where students can share their opinions, concerns, and suggestions.

4

WHAT'S HAPPENING AROUND US?

Organize sessions about current events, international news, and important topics in an interesting and simple way.

5

MODEL UNITED NATIONS (MUN)

Give students the opportunity to represent countries, discuss global problems, negotiate, and find solutions.

6

STUDENT VOICE SESSIONS

Organize discussions about education, social media, bullying, AI, youth opportunities, and other topics that matter to students.
